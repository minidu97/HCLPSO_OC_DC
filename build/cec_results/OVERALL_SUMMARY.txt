HCLPSO-OC - OVERALL SUMMARY ACROSS ALL DIMENSIONS
============================================================================================================================================

This experiment tested the algorithm on:
- 29 CEC2017 functions (F1-F30, excluding F2)
- 4 dimensions: D = 10, 30, 50, 100
- 51 independent runs per function

Metrics tracked:
1. Calculation Accuracy: Mean, Std, Median, Best, Worst
2. Convergence Speed: CFEs (function evaluations to converge)
3. Success Rate: Percentage of runs that converged
4. Calculation Time: CTs (seconds)

Results are organized by dimension in separate subdirectories.
